Welcome to E-Vault

Welcome to Sphoorthy Engineering College's comprehensive digital library – E-Vault: A Safe Repository for Knowledge. Our platform provides students and faculty with secure access to thousands of academic resources, including course lecture notes, textbooks, lab manuals, and research papers.

Our digital repository ensures that educational materials are conveniently available while maintaining academic integrity and strict location-based geofence protections.
